Our approach

Outdoor materials for people who care how a place holds together — from the first layer to the final line.

Source

Mill-direct, single origin

We buy woven PP from two mills we've audited ourselves, not from aggregators. Same looms, same yarn, every batch.

Test

Weighed before dispatch

A cut sample from every batch is weighed against the labelled GSM. Under-spec fabric is rejected, not discounted.

Move

48-hour dispatch

Stock lives in our Punjab warehouse, not on a supplier's promise. Orders confirmed by 2 pm move the same week, usually within two days.

Back

Replaced, not argued

If a roll fails on GSM or tears within the covered period, we replace it. Your site crew shouldn't carry our risk.
